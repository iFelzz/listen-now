FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading music with Melodyxx.

How do I convert and download YouTube to MP3?

Type a song title or artist into the search box above, or paste any YouTube video link. Pick your desired audio quality (128k, 192k, or 320k), then click Download. Melodyxx instantly pipes the converted MP3 file directly to your browser.

Are album covers and ID3 tags embedded in the audio?

Yes. Melodyxx automatically queries Apple Music to match track titles and embed official 800x800 square cover artwork, song title, artist, album name, and release year directly into the ID3v2 metadata frames.

Can I download multiple songs or entire albums at once?

Yes. Click the "Artists" search tab to explore discographies. Check the boxes next to the songs you want—or click "Select All"—then hit "Download Selected" to sequentially download the entire batch.

What is the highest audio quality bitrate available?

Melodyxx offers 320kbps (best high-definition fidelity), 192kbps (optimal balance of size and quality), and 128kbps (lightweight data saving). You can easily switch quality using the global toggle at the top of the search results.
